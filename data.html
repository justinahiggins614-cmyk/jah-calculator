WHAT THE EQUATION ARCHIVE IS

Every equation is solved once by this calculator, published as a JAH-EQ record (official within the Signature/JAH system) — then just retrieved, never solved again. The archive is the calculator's permanent solved-once memory, marching to 1,000,000 records. "Official JAH-EQ record" means a canonical record generated and stored by this calculator — official within the Signature/JAH system.

THE JAH-EQ ID SCHEME

Each record carries a permanent serial ID of the form JAH-EQ-######## — the prefix JAH-EQ- followed by an 8-digit zero-padded number assigned in publication order (record JAH-EQ-00000001 is the first ever published). IDs are never reused and never reassigned; a record's ID is its permanent address, e.g. index.html?tab=eq&eq=JAH-EQ-00000001.

SOLVED-AT-GENERATION + NUMERICALLY VERIFIED

Each record is solved at the moment it is generated, and the solution is then checked numerically — the check field of every record carries the verification (e.g. substituting the solution back into the equation). Records are published only after the check passes, and each record stores:

FieldMeaning
idPermanent JAH-EQ serial (e.g. JAH-EQ-00000001)

typeEquation family: linear, quadratic, system2, trig, power, log, exp, evaluate, percent, derivative, integral, geometry

solver_versionWhich solver build produced the record

VERSIONING

Records are immutable once published: an existing JAH-EQ record is never edited in place. If the solver improves, new records carry the new solver_version; old records keep theirs, so the archive stays a faithful history of what was solved and when. Identity is by canonical form — syntactically different but mathematically equivalent inputs get their own record unless canonically identical.

PRIVACY & DATA STORAGE

  • Calculations, simulations, and lab readings never leave your device. There is no account, no analytics, and no calculation data is uploaded anywhere.
  • Solve records and the lab notebook live in your browser's localStorage only. They persist across refreshes and restarts, are erased if you clear browser data, and do not follow you to another device.
  • Read-aloud is the one exception: tapping a voice button sends the text being spoken to your voice provider — first your browser's built-in speech synthesis, then (if unavailable) the ResponsiveVoice or Google text-to-speech services — so the audio can be generated. Voice use is always your explicit tap, never automatic.
  • Downloaded files (CSV, TXT, equation records) are plain data files, not executables. The downloadable equation chunks contain JSON records, never code that runs on open.

VERSIONS

  • Engine: jah-calc-engine-1.0 — hand-written expression parser (no eval), quadratic/cubic solvers, unit converter, paradox checker, projection engine, seeded simulations, virtual lab.
  • Solver: jah-eq-solver-1.0 — the generator that solves each equation once at publication; every record carries its solver version.
  • Record schema: jah-eq-schema-1.0 — ID, type, input, normalized input, exact/approximate result, verification, assumptions, solver/engine/schema versions, canonical form + hash, archive status, seed, source, stable URL, record SHA-256 hash, timestamp.
  • Archive revision: published in api.json as archive_revision with archive_generated_at; records are immutable once published and never renumbered.

GROWTH & EXPORTS

  • The archive grows by +2,000 records every 2 hours toward 1,000,000.
  • CSV export: the full equation catalog as CSV (id, n, type, title, equation, steps, solution, check, solver_version, engine_version, schema_version, canonical, canonical_hash, record_hash, archive_status, seed, source, stable_url, ts). UTF-8, fields quoted where needed; SHA-256 hashes are per-record.
