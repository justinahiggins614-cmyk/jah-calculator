PROJECTILE MOTION

Formulas: range = v²·sin(2θ)/g · height = (v·sinθ)²/2g · time = 2v·sinθ/g

What this proves: with no air resistance, uniform gravity, and equal launch/landing height, 45° maximizes range; the trajectory is a parabola under those conditions.

OHM'S LAW

Formula: I = V/R · P = V·I

What this proves (ideal Ohmic/resistive model): current is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance. Real components like diodes and filaments do not follow this model.

IDEAL GAS

Formula: P = nRT/V, R = 0.08206 L·atm/(mol·K)

What this proves: for fixed amount of gas and fixed volume, increasing temperature raises pressure; for fixed amount and temperature, decreasing volume raises pressure.
